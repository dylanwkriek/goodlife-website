Cookies and Site Storage

Last updated: 28 September 2026

We reviewed the website code on this date. It does not deliberately set cookies or use advertising or analytics scripts. Its gallery and enquiry functions do not require browser cookies or local storage. For that reason, there is currently no optional-cookie setting to accept or reject.

Our hosting provider and services you open from the site, such as WhatsApp, may use their own technical storage under their policies. A quotation request is sent to GoodLife’s lead system; this is explained in the Privacy Notice. Campaign and referral details can accompany an enquiry even though the site does not set a tracking cookie.

If analytics, advertising tags or optional cookies are added later, we will review this page and the need for a consent choice before enabling them.
