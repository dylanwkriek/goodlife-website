Privacy Notice

Last updated: 28 September 2026

GoodLife Awnings & Pavings uses information you provide to respond to enquiries and prepare quotations. Grant Marks is the contact for GoodLife. DwK Business Development Group helps capture and manage enquiries through its business hub.

What we collect

If you request a quote, we collect your name, phone number, selected service and project details. We also record the source of the enquiry, such as campaign tags, referring page and the website origin, and the version of the form consent you accepted. When you choose Call, WhatsApp or Email, the site records the type of contact action and a time associated with it; an anonymous contact action may appear in the lead pipeline.

Why and how we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, arrange work or a quotation, and keep track of enquiries sent to GoodLife. The quote form sends the information to the lead system and opens a prepared WhatsApp message to Grant; sending that WhatsApp message is your choice. Direct contact buttons open your phone, WhatsApp or email app. The site does not ask for consent to unrelated marketing.

Who receives it

Grant and authorised people assisting GoodLife with enquiries may access the information. The website uses a Supabase-hosted lead service connected to the DwK business hub. If you choose WhatsApp or email, those services process what you send under their own terms. Hosting and service providers may handle technical data needed to run the site. The lead system is configured with a project in the European region, so personal information submitted through the form may be stored outside South Africa.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for as long as reasonably needed to manage the request, business records and applicable legal obligations. GoodLife and DwK are finalising a specific retention and deletion schedule. You may ask about a record or request its correction or deletion using the contact details below, subject to applicable obligations.

Your choices and rights

You may contact GoodLife to ask what personal information is held about you, request correction or deletion where applicable, or object to processing. You can also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.
