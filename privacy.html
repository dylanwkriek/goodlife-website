Privacy Notice

Last updated: 4 November 2026

GoodLife Awnings & Pavings is the trading name of Grant Marks, a sole proprietor. GoodLife uses information you provide to respond to enquiries and prepare quotations. Grant is GoodLife’s Information Officer and the contact for privacy requests. DwK Business Development Group receives website enquiries through its business hub and forwards them to Grant. Grant handles customer follow-up, quotations, pricing and service delivery; DwK does not prepare quotations or set GoodLife’s prices.

What we collect

If you request a quote, we collect your name, phone number, selected service and project details. We also record the source of the enquiry, such as campaign tags, referring page and the website origin, and the version of the form consent you accepted. When you choose Call, WhatsApp or Email, the site records the type of contact action and a time associated with it; an anonymous contact action may appear in the lead pipeline.

Why and how we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, arrange work or a quotation, and keep track of enquiries sent to GoodLife. The quote form sends the information to the lead system and opens a prepared WhatsApp message to Grant; sending that WhatsApp message is your choice. Direct contact buttons open your phone, WhatsApp or email app. The site does not ask for consent to unrelated marketing.

Who receives it

Dylan at DwK is currently the only person with access to GoodLife’s enquiry records in the DwK Hub. Dylan forwards enquiries to Grant so that Grant can respond. GoodLife and DwK have a written arrangement covering the forwarding of enquiries. The website uses a Supabase-hosted lead service connected to the DwK business hub. If you choose WhatsApp or email, those services process what you send under their own terms. Hosting and service providers may handle technical data needed to run the site. Personal information submitted through the form may be stored outside South Africa. The specific database hosting region is being verified.

How long we keep it

When an enquiry is confirmed as unsuccessful or no longer progressing, its enquiry record is deleted from the DwK Hub and the related WhatsApp conversation is deleted. For customers whose jobs proceed, routine enquiry details and related conversations are kept for three months after the job is completed, then deleted unless further work is being arranged or another lawful reason requires retention. Invoices, financial records and records needed for legal obligations, ongoing work or disputes may need to be retained separately for longer. These practices concern records held by GoodLife and DwK; they do not control copies held by customers or the separate retention practices of service providers. You may ask about a record or request its correction or deletion using the contact details below, subject to applicable obligations.

Your choices and rights

You may contact GoodLife to ask what personal information is held about you, request correction or deletion where applicable, or object to processing. You can also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.
