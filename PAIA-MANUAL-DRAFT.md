## 4. Requesting access
Use the prescribed Form 2 available from the Regulator's PAIA page and send it to Grant at the email above. Identify the record, the requested form of access, contact details, any representative capacity, and the right to be exercised or protected and why the record is required for that right. Grant may request necessary verification of identity or authority.
Prescribed fees, exemptions and lawful grounds for refusal may apply. Grant should advise the requester of applicable fees and the outcome using the prescribed procedure; this manual does not impose additional invented fees.
Requests are ordinarily decided within 30 days, subject to lawful extensions and notice. If access is refused or no response is provided within the applicable period, remedies include a complaint to the Information Regulator or an application to court as permitted by PAIA. The Regulator's current forms and guidance explain the applicable complaint time limits. A private body does not use the public-body internal appeal process.

As confirmed by the owner to DwK: unsuccessful or no-longer-progressing enquiries are deleted from the Hub and related WhatsApp conversations are deleted. This is not a three-month deletion rule for invoices, financial records or records needed for legal obligations or disputes.
